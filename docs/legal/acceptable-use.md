# SehatAI Care Connect — Acceptable Use Policy

> **DRAFT — REQUIRES COUNSEL REVIEW BEFORE ANY COMMERCIAL LAUNCH**  
> **Jurisdiction: PK (draft) / AF (draft)**  
> **Version:** 2026.10-draft · **Effective Date:** October 2026

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited activities and behavioral standards for all users—patients, doctors, administrators, and staff—interacting with SehatAI Care Connect.

---

## 2. Prohibited Clinical & Medical Conduct

1. **Controlled Substance Solicitation:** Users may not request, demand, or attempt to procure prescriptions for scheduled narcotics, controlled stimulants, addictive benzodiazepines, or abortifacients through telemedicine.
2. **Impersonation:** Users may not impersonate any licensed physician, healthcare practitioner, or other individual. Practicing medicine without a valid license is a criminal offense under Pakistan and Afghan law.
3. **Emergency Circumvention:** Users may not utilize the Platform to delay emergency medical attention for unstable or life-threatening medical conditions.

---

## 3. Communication & Behavioral Rules

1. **Abusive or Threatening Behavior:** Harassment, verbal abuse, sexual harassment, profanity, or threats directed toward doctors or platform support staff will result in immediate permanent account termination and referral to legal authorities.
2. **Fraudulent Reviews:** Submitting fabricated, purchased, retaliatory, or non-consultation-based reviews or ratings is strictly prohibited.
3. **Commercial Spam:** Using consultation messaging, community channels, or doctor directories to broadcast commercial advertisements, unverified home remedies, or pyramid schemes is forbidden.

---

## 4. Technical Security & Platform Integrity

Users and third parties must not:
1. Attempt unauthorized access to consultation records, medical notes, or attachments belonging to other users (IDOR violations).
2. Decompile, reverse engineer, intercept network traffic, or probe vulnerabilities of the Care Connect API or signaling servers.
3. Introduce viruses, trojans, ransomware, or malicious files into medical document uploads.
4. Exploit race conditions or double-spend vulnerabilities against the consultation booking or payment ledger.
5. Deploy automated scrapers, web spiders, or automated bots against doctor directories or messaging systems.

---

## 5. Violations, Penalties & Reporting

Violations of this Policy will result in graduated or immediate enforcement actions:
- Formal administrative warning.
- Suspension or forfeiture of pending payouts (for bad-faith provider breaches).
- Permanent account deactivation and blacklisting.
- Formal complaint to medical licensing authorities (PM&DC) and criminal law enforcement.

To report violations: `compliance@sehatai.health`
