# SehatAI Care Connect — Clinical Disclaimer & Safety Boundary

## 1. Primary Clinical Boundary

**SEHATAI CARE CONNECT AND ITS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEMS DO NOT PROVIDE MEDICAL DIAGNOSES, PRESCRIBE MEDICATIONS, OR DELIVER CLINICAL MEDICAL ADVICE.**

All clinical care, medical opinions, evaluations, and prescription decisions are rendered exclusively by licensed, independent human medical practitioners verified by the platform.

---

## 2. Role and Limitations of Artificial Intelligence (AI) (§31, §39)

The SehatAI platform utilizes automated intelligence modules for specific assistive functions:
1. **Intake Triage & Routing:** The deterministic safety pipeline categorizes patient complaints into clinical acuity bands (e.g., ROUTINE, URGENT, EMERGENCY) and suggests appropriate doctor specialties. This routing is educational and triage-supportive; it does not constitute a diagnostic pronouncement.
2. **AI Clinical Handoff (§31):** The AI-generated intake summary prepared for the attending doctor is explicitly labeled:
   `"AI-ASSISTED CLINICAL SUMMARY — REQUIRES INDEPENDENT CLINICAL VERIFICATION"`.
   The doctor must review, verify, or edit this summary before adopting any portion into the medical record.
3. **AI Never Prescribes (§39):** Under no circumstance can the AI issue, finalize, or submit a medical prescription. The prescription drafting tool provides automated drug-interaction safety warnings, but only a verified, licensed human doctor can digitally sign and issue an authorized prescription.

---

## 3. Scope of Telemedicine Practice

1. **Non-Emergency Standard:** Telemedicine is strictly designed for non-emergent, elective, primary care, and follow-up medical consultations.
2. **Physical Exam Limitation:** Telemedicine lacks the ability to perform physical auscultation, palpation, neurological reflex testing, or diagnostic palpation. It relies on observational assessment, clinical history, and patient-provided data.
3. **Escalation to In-Person Facility:** If at any point during a consultation the doctor suspects an acute coronary syndrome, stroke, internal hemorrhage, unstable fracture, severe infection, or surgical abdomen, the doctor is ethically and legally obligated to terminate the remote session and advise the patient to immediately present to the nearest hospital emergency room.

---

## 4. Patient Responsibility

The patient acknowledges responsibility for:
1. Providing an accurate, truthful, and comprehensive account of symptoms, past medical history, current medications, drug allergies, and pregnancy status.
2. Following the explicit advice of the attending physician, including instructions to seek emergency care or undergo in-person laboratory testing.
3. Not modifying medication doses or discontinuing vital therapies based solely on automated platform tools.
