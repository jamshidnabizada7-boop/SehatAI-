# SehatAI Care Connect — Cancellation Policy

> **Jurisdiction: PK (draft) / AF (draft)**  
> **Policy Version:** 2026.10-initial · **Effective Date:** October 2026

---

## 1. Principles of Cancellation

To maintain an orderly, reliable telemedicine service for both patients and healthcare providers, SehatAI Care Connect enforces clear rules regarding appointment and consultation cancellations.

---

## 2. Patient-Initiated Cancellations

1. **Before Doctor Acceptance:**
   - A patient may cancel a pending consultation request at any time prior to the doctor accepting the consultation.
   - The consultation transitions to `CANCELLED` and triggers an automatic 100% full refund of the consultation fee.

2. **Scheduled Video / Audio Appointments:**
   - **More than 24 hours prior to appointment:** Full 100% refund.
   - **Less than 24 hours prior to appointment:** 50% partial refund. The remaining 50% is credited to the doctor to compensate for the unfillable reserved clinical time.
   - **After Appointment Start Time / Missed Appointment:** If a patient fails to join the call within the 10-minute grace period, the session is deemed a patient no-show; no refund is issued.

3. **Text Consultations:**
   - Once a text consultation is accepted by the doctor and enters the active messaging window, cancellation is not permitted as the clinical review has commenced.

---

## 3. Doctor-Initiated Cancellations

1. **Prior to Consultation:** If an unforeseen medical emergency or urgent hospital commitment prevents the doctor from attending, the doctor may decline or cancel the consultation.
2. **Mandatory Patient Compensation:** In all cases where the doctor cancels or fails to attend a scheduled session, the patient receives an automatic 100% full refund immediately.
3. **Doctor Reliability Metrics:** Frequent doctor cancellations adversely impact doctor ranking scores and may lead to administrative investigation or temporary suspension from the marketplace directory.

---

## 4. Platform Auto-Expiry (§47, §48)

- Consultation requests that remain unaccepted by a doctor within the designated response window (15 minutes for instant requests; 24 hours for standard requests) automatically transition to `EXPIRED`.
- The patient's captured payment is immediately and automatically refunded in full.
