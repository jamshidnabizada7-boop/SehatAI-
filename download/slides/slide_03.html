A trilingual, offline-capable AI health companion that puts safety first

SehatAI is a Progressive Web App that provides immediate, evidence-grounded health guidance in English, Urdu (اردو Nastaliq), and Roman Urdu. Unlike generic chatbots, it uses a deterministic-first safety architecture — emergencies are detected in under 100 milliseconds and bypass the LLM entirely, displaying pre-verified WHO action cards with one-tap dialing to 1122, 1166, and 115.
